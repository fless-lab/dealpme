# DealPME V3.1 — VDR Intelligence Upgrade

## Scope
This release upgrades the V3 VDR from a controlled document room into a diligence intelligence workspace.

## New product surfaces
- Cockpit de diligence
- Documents / secure viewer
- DealLens IA document-scoped and room-scoped
- Issue Radar
- Evidence Map
- Q&R Workbench
- Engagement Pulse
- Audit
- Access & Clean Team

## Data additions across PT-001 to PT-012
- 336 per-document AI intelligence records
- diligence coverage data by domain
- evidence graphs linking documents, risks, Q&R and transaction metrics
- financial tie-out examples
- version-intelligence examples
- AI redaction proposals requiring human publication
- access matrix and Clean Team policy
- room-level DealLens prompts and policy
- 456 VDR interaction contracts

## Security and legal corrections
The implementation intentionally excludes unsupported DealPME claims such as SOC/ISO certification and excludes absolute anti-capture wording. The reference uses deterrence, attribution and traceability language consistent with the DealPME VDR threat model.

## AI governance
DealLens is read-only. Permission filtering occurs before retrieval. Clean Team and expert scopes are inherited by AI. Revocation propagates to AI retrieval. Material answers require citations to authorized evidence.
