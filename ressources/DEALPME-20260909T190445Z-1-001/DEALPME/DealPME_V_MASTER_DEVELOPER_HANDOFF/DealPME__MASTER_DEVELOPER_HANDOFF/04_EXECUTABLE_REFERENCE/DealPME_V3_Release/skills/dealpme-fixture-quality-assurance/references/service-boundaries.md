# Service-boundary rules v3

## Platform-wide
DealPME orchestrates workflow and evidence. It does not become the transaction party, regulated adviser, business valuator, legal/tax/accounting adviser, or holder/transmitter of transaction consideration in the governed v0 perimeter.

## Deal-Ready
Pre-flight score may route/prioritize. Award/revoke requires human institutional decision evidence.

## Deal-Experts
Expert acts within a mandate/file/folder scope, with expiring access. Conflict blocks or revokes assignment.

## VDR/Q&A
Detailed access requires the declared gate. Download may be disabled. Watermark/audit evidence should be explicit. Revocation must be auditable.

## Alerte & Rebond
Protected distress/conciliation-linked cases use elevated confidentiality and closed/invite-only access where applicable.

## LegalTech OHADA
Documents are drafting aids requiring qualified professional review. Employment contract work is national labour law, not an OHADA uniform-act document.

## Conformité & Fiscalité
Partner/API failures stay failed/pending. Never fabricate certificate/filing success.

## Deal-Connect
Contact exchange follows consent. Channel truth matters: audio fallback is not a successful video session.

## Guichet Diaspora
Cross-border FX/repatriation/authorization issues route to qualified bank/counsel review; DealPME does not issue autonomous regulated advice.
