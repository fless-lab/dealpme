# Product surfaces

## Cockpit
Decision-oriented progress: diligence coverage, critical gaps, unresolved issues, Q&R, recent activity and next review priorities. Do not label coverage as an audit conclusion.

## Documents / Viewer
Desktop default: diligence taxonomy left, document list/viewer centre, DealLens contextual rail right. Synchronize document selection with metadata, version, risks, Q&R, evidence and AI context.

## DealLens IA
Modes: document-scoped and room-scoped. Required answer anatomy: answer, confidence, citations, potential issues, related evidence, open questions and limitation.

## Issue Radar
Structured issue register: severity, probability, status, source, owner, impact, evidence, Q&R, mitigation and due date.

## Evidence Map
Graph of Documents, Facts, Metrics, Risks, Q&A, ContractTerms and DealReadyGaps. Use explicit relations such as SUPPORTS, CONTRADICTS, RAISES, RESOLVES and EVIDENCES.

## Q&R Workbench
Support Question Author, Question Coordinator, Answer Coordinator, Expert and Approver patterns. DealLens may draft but never auto-submit.

## Engagement Pulse
Descriptive buyer activity: logins, document coverage, review velocity, Q&A, senior participation and recent activity. Do not claim predictive closing probability without a validated model.

## Audit
Append-only event evidence for sensitive actions. Never log confidential document bodies or sensitive identity data in plaintext observability logs.

## Access & Clean Team
Named groups, folder/document grants, expiration, reason, download policy and AI scope. Clean Team and expert access must be explicit and auditable.
